penguin-forge

A multiplayer penguin world running on a from-scratch HTTP/WebSocket framework — built only on Node's net module, zero dependencies. The framework's own dashboards are the game's live control room.

🎮

Play the world

Pick a name, waddle around as a penguin, hop between rooms, chat, and play arcade mini-games — live, with everyone in every tab at once. Open it in a few tabs to see real-time multiplayer.

Enter the world ▸
⚙️

The framework

fs-forge: the creative HTTP/1.1 + WebSocket framework powering all of this. Filesystem routing, a tagged-template DSL, a live API console, hot reload, and an in-browser route builder.

Open the showcase ▸

Best demo: open /play and /_data side by side — join the world in one, watch the penguin appear in the roster in the other, then walk between rooms and watch its room update live.
